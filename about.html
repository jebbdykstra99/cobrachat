About cobrachat

cobrachat is a room for people who talk cobras and related snakes with care — species notes, myth busts, field lore, and stories. Hoods up.

Wildlife education over ownership. We are not a pet store, not a husbandry how-to for dangerous animals, and not a place to flex captures. Public-range and conservation talk beats private ownership theater.

Guest can browse. Sign-in is for a handle and posts. Direct messages may be limited in this preview.
