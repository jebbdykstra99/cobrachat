About cobrachat

cobrachat is a room for people who talk cobras and related snakes with care: species notes, myth busts, field lore, and stories. Hoods up.

Wildlife education over ownership. SubX is not a pet store, not a husbandry how-to for dangerous animals, and not a place to flex captures. Public-range and conservation talk beats private ownership theater.

Guest can browse. Sign-in is for a handle and posts. Direct messages may be limited.

This site is a public beta. You can read without an account. Posting in the room requires an account, and every account holder must be 18 or older. Public pages (except /report.html) are open to search indexing.
