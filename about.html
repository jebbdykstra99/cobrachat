About cobrachat

cobrachat is a preview room for king cobras and the stories around them. Hoods up. This is wildlife education — species cards, myth vs fact, and public conservation lore. It is not a pet store.

Wildlife education over ownership. Wild animals stay wild. The Care nest is education, not sales, and not husbandry instructions for illegal keeping. Licensed rehab and zoo education are the public path. Never handle wild cobras.

  • Spitting nest is behavior overview only — no how-to. Medical issues go to a doctor.
  • This is not first-aid advice, not a field guide for capture, and not a replacement for licensed wildlife professionals.
